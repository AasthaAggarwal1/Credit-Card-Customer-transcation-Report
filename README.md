# 💳 Credit Card Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Credit Card Analytics Dashboard** is an end-to-end Business Intelligence project developed using **Power BI**, **MySQL**, **Power Query**, and **DAX**. This project transforms raw credit card customer and transaction data into meaningful business insights through interactive and visually appealing dashboards.

The solution consists of two interconnected dashboards:

* **Credit Card Transaction Dashboard** – Focuses on financial performance, transaction analysis, revenue trends, expenditure categories, payment methods, and card performance.
* **Credit Card Customer Dashboard** – Provides insights into customer demographics, income distribution, education, profession, marital status, customer satisfaction, and revenue contribution across different customer segments.

The dashboards enable users to monitor key business metrics, identify customer spending patterns, evaluate revenue performance, and support data-driven decision-making through interactive visualizations and dynamic filtering.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze overall revenue, transaction amount, interest earned, and customer satisfaction.
* Monitor weekly revenue and transaction trends.
* Understand customer behavior based on demographics.
* Identify high-value customer segments.
* Analyze spending patterns across different expenditure categories.
* Compare performance by payment mode and card category.
* Generate actionable insights for business growth and customer retention.
